Linux Driver - Enablement and Integration
=========================================

his document provides an overview of Linux driver enablement and integration in PHYTEC BSPs. It covers driver identification, kernel configuration, device-tree integration, driver binding, probe operations, and subsystem interfaces. It also explains driver verification, troubleshooting, and the integration of driver customizations into Yocto-based BSPs.
