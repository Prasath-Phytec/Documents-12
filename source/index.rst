Pinmux Registers and - Electrical Configuration
===============================================

This document explains pin multiplexing (pinmux), register configuration, and electrical settings in PHYTEC boards. It covers mux selection, pad-control fields, pull-up and pull-down configuration, drive strength, slew rate, and voltage domains for NXP i.MX and TI K3 processors. It also describes how to configure and verify pinctrl states, troubleshoot common electrical issues, and ensure reliable hardware operation by following the SoC reference manual and PHYTEC board design.
